Our Craftsmanship Story

Bringing Anime Props
From Screen to Hand

3D Otaku was created by passionate anime enthusiasts and additive manufacturing engineers. We bridge the gap between digital anime imagination and physical cosplay craftsmanship by producing high-detail, non-sharp 3D printed props.

All blades, daggers, katanas, and weapons produced by 3D Otaku are manufactured exclusively as decorative non-functional display items and cosplay accessories. They feature blunted plastic edges, zero metal sharpness, and lightweight structural fill suitable for convention security checks.
